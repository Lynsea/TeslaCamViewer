Start in your browser

From drive to footage in three steps.

Your browser asks you to choose a folder. The viewer reads the files locally; no video upload is part of this workflow.

  1. 01

    Connect your drive

    Insert the USB drive or open the folder where your TeslaCam recordings are stored.

  2. 02

    Select the TeslaCam folder

    Open the viewer in Chrome or Edge, then grant access to your TeslaCam folder or a folder containing it.

  3. 03

    Choose an event

    Browse SavedClips, SentryClips, or RecentClips and review the available camera views.

Common questions

Before you open a folder.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. The viewer reads video files from the folder you choose in your browser. Optional map and weather features can contact third-party services using location data.

Which browser should I use?

Use a current version of Chrome or Edge. The folder access feature is not supported in Safari or Firefox.

Can I open Sentry Mode clips?

Yes. Select the TeslaCam folder containing SentryClips, or select that folder directly.
