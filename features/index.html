The viewer

Every angle. One place to review it.

TeslaCam reads compatible Tesla dashcam folders in your browser and aligns the camera clips that belong to the same event. Use the tools you need without uploading the videos.

Open the viewer →Setup guide

Review footage

Follow the event across cameras.

The viewer is built around a shared timeline, so you can inspect a moment without juggling separate files and playback windows.

Camera views

Synchronized playback

Play front, rear, and side footage together. The available views depend on the recordings in the selected event.

Navigation

Events in context

Browse SavedClips, SentryClips, and RecentClips; filter events by type and date; and jump between recordings.

Timeline

Move through a moment

Pause, seek, change playback speed, or step through frames when you need a closer look.

Work with the recording

Keep what matters from a clip.

Capture frames, mark a segment, or export a recording for your own review. Export availability and limits depend on the viewer's access settings.

Capture

Save a screenshot

Capture the current camera view when a still frame helps document what you saw.

Export

Mark and export

Set in and out points to focus on the part of a recording you want to keep.

Optional data

Maps and telemetry

When a recording contains supported location or telemetry data, the viewer can display additional context. Map and weather features may call external services.

Know before you start

What the viewer needs.

A supported browser and your footage

Use Chrome or Edge and choose a TeslaCam folder stored on a connected drive or your computer. Safari and Firefox cannot provide the folder access this viewer uses. Some advanced features download models on demand or have usage limits.
